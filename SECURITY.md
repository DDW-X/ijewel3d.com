# Security Policy & Responsible Disclosure

## Reporting a Vulnerability or Rights Inquiry

This repository is maintained by **DDW-X** as an educational and scientific reverse-engineering research case study focusing on client-side 3D graphics, WebGL2 performance, and WebAssembly asset pipelines.

If you believe you have found a security vulnerability in any of the tooling, instrumentation scripts, or architectural demonstrations in this repository, or if you represent a copyright holder with inquiries regarding intellectual property or rights management, please follow our responsible disclosure procedure:

### Point of Contact
- **Lead Researcher:** DDW-X (Cybersecurity Specialist & Low-Level Systems Analyst)
- **Email:** [ddw.x.dev@gmail.com](mailto:ddw.x.dev@gmail.com)
- **PGP/GPG Key:** Available upon request for encrypted communications.

### Guidelines for Responsible Disclosure
1. Please do not open public GitHub issues for sensitive security vulnerabilities or rights-related discussions.
2. Provide a descriptive summary, steps to reproduce, and any relevant proof-of-concept details in your email.
3. We will acknowledge receipt of your report within 48 business hours and provide a timeline for triage and resolution.
4. If a vulnerability is confirmed in any custom tooling or proof-of-concept code, a security patch will be pushed with appropriate credit to the reporter.

Thank you for helping keep our research and open-source tooling safe and compliant.
